HEALTHY SNACK IDEAS IN DUBAI

Sometimes the best snack is the one made from familiar ingredients. D8 Bark starts with Medjool dates, then adds a spread, chocolate, crunch and finish in a format designed to snap and share.

A date snack for active days

Keep a chilled bark sheet at home or work and break off the amount you want. The format works for a coffee break, an afternoon snack, a gym bag treat after training or a shareable dessert.

Choose what goes on it

Build your own with peanut butter, pistachio butter, almond butter or tahini, then choose dark, milk or white chocolate and your favorite toppings.

Fresh in Dubai

D8 Bark is made in small batches in Dubai. Orders are requested first so ingredients, timing and UAE delivery can be confirmed before production.
