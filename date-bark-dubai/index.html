DATE BARK IN DUBAI

D8 Bark makes fresh Medjool date bark in Dubai as a full sheet you can snap into pieces. Each sheet layers pressed dates with a spread, chocolate, crunch and finish.

Build your own date bark

Choose peanut butter, pistachio butter, almond butter or tahini. Add dark, milk or white chocolate, then choose your crunch and finish. Custom orders are confirmed before production so each sheet is made for the person ordering it.

A fresh UAE snack format

Date bark sits between a date snack, chocolate treat and shareable dessert. Keep it chilled and break off a piece for coffee, an afternoon snack, after training or when you want something sweet.

Dubai and UAE delivery

D8 is made in Dubai. Dubai delivery is available by request, and other UAE locations are confirmed based on courier timing and temperature handling.
