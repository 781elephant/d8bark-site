ORDER TERMS

Submitting a request through this website is not a final paid order. D8 Bark confirms ingredient availability, quantity, total price, payment method, delivery fee and timing before production begins.

Small batch availability

Ingredients and production capacity may vary. A requested combination is not guaranteed until D8 Bark confirms it.

Allergens

Products may contain or come into contact with nuts, peanuts, sesame, milk and other allergens. Customizing a bark does not guarantee that it is free from allergens.

Storage and delivery

Chocolate can soften quickly in UAE heat. Keep bark chilled after delivery. Delivery timing and fees depend on location and are confirmed before an order is accepted.

Changes and cancellations

Because products are made in small batches, changes or cancellations may not be possible once production has started. Any applicable terms are confirmed with the final order.
