FAQ

THE BARK BASICS.

What exactly is date bark?

Date bark is a layered snack made with pressed dates, spread, chocolate and toppings. We make it as a full sheet so you can snap it into pieces.

Can I really build my own?

Yes. Choose one spread, one chocolate, up to two crunch options and one finish. Send the build as a request and we will confirm ingredients, price and delivery.

How big is a sheet?

Our launch format is a shareable bark sheet packed flat in a shallow box. Exact finished weight can vary slightly because each sheet is made in small batches.

Do you deliver across the UAE?

D8 is made in Dubai. Dubai delivery is available by request. Other UAE locations are confirmed individually based on courier timing and temperature handling.

How should I store it?

Keep it chilled in its sealed pack. UAE heat can soften chocolate quickly, so refrigerate your bark after delivery and only take out what you plan to enjoy.

What about allergens?

D8 recipes can contain nuts, peanuts, sesame, milk and other allergens. Customizing a build does not make it allergen free. Always tell us about any allergy before ordering.

Do you take event or office orders?

Yes. Use the order request and add the number of sheets, date and event details. We can quote larger small batch orders separately.
