PRIVACY

When you send an order request, you may provide your name, email, phone number, delivery area, requested date and order notes. D8 Bark uses this information to respond to your request, confirm your order and coordinate delivery.

Order information

Only provide information needed to process your request. Do not include payment card details in an email or WhatsApp order request.

Website data

This launch site does not require an account. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this notice should be updated before those tools are activated.
